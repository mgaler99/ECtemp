ChatGPT

Personal Free, Plus or Pro accounts

Staff only

Can students use it directly?

No — students do not use this tool directly.

Age or account needed

13+

Under 18: parent or legal guardian permission

What it does

Generative AI for brainstorming, drafting, summarising, question generation and—depending on the plan—file or image analysis.

01

Suitable uses

  • Staff planning and preparation.
  • Drafting research questions or search terms for teacher review.
  • Brainstorming or summarising public, generic material.
  • Teacher-led demonstrations using checked output.
02

Do not use for

  • Directing or requiring students to use personal ChatGPT accounts.
  • Entering personal, sensitive or confidential information.
  • Replacing authoritative research, sources or citations.
  • Generating final assessed work on a student’s behalf.
03

Conditions of use

  • Use only public or generic information.
  • Check outputs before sharing them with students.
  • Verify claims using approved, authoritative sources.
  • Turn off model training or use Temporary Chat.
04

Why these rules apply

  • Personal accounts are not administered or controlled by the College.
  • Users must be 13+; users under 18 require parent or guardian permission.
  • Individual data and model-training settings cannot be consistently controlled.
  • Outputs may be inaccurate and require independent verification.
Account scope
Consumer Free, Plus or Pro account — not College managed
Age rule
13+; users under 18 require parent or legal guardian permission
Reviewed
29 July 2026

Scope matters: this decision applies to personal Free, Plus and Pro accounts. A College-contracted or managed ChatGPT service would require a separate assessment.

Tool and account type

Claude

Consumer Free, Pro or Max accounts

Staff only

Can students use it directly?

No — consumer accounts require users to be 18+.

Age or account needed

18+

Consumer accounts

What it does

Generative AI for drafting, critique, structured analysis, coding and long-document work. Available features and usage limits vary by plan.

01

Suitable uses

  • Staff planning, drafting and critique.
  • Structuring questions, arguments or alternative perspectives.
  • Working with public, generic or staff-authored material.
  • Teacher-led demonstrations using checked output.
02

Do not use for

  • Creating accounts for students or asking students to use Claude directly.
  • Entering personal, sensitive or confidential information.
  • Uploading student work, results, correspondence or internal records.
  • Treating Claude output as authoritative evidence or a final decision.
03

Conditions of use

  • Use only public, generic, invented or fully de-identified material.
  • Independently verify accuracy, currency and completeness.
  • Turn off model improvement or use an Incognito Chat.
  • Check suitability, copyright and representation before sharing output.
04

Why these rules apply

  • Consumer accounts are not College-administered, even when a College email address is used.
  • Anthropic’s consumer terms require users to be at least 18.
  • Consumer materials may be used for model improvement unless the user opts out.
  • Outputs may contain material inaccuracies and require independent confirmation.
Account scope
Consumer Free, Pro or Max account — personal or College email address
Age rule
18+ for Anthropic consumer services
Reviewed
29 July 2026

Scope matters: using a College email address does not by itself create an enterprise or College-managed account. Claude for Work, Team or Enterprise would require a separate assessment of the contracted configuration.
